AI BEHIND THE SCENES. LEARNING ON PAPER.

Personalized by AI.
Learned on paper.

Children learn with pencils, paper, and hands-on activities. They do not need a screen. Counting, drawing, writing, and working with paper materials give them opportunities to practice handwriting and hands-on skills alongside each lesson.

Behind the scenes, ANGI builds an understanding of your child’s learning path. Within a complete course, our AI generates each lesson around their profile and progress, then adapts what comes next using uploaded homework and your observations.

Lessons and homework stay on paper. Parents can print materials and share feedback by uploading completed work, answering simple yes-or-no questions, or doing both.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

A little more to know.

Who is Inksmore for?+

We’re starting with math for children ages 2 to 6, supported by a parent or educator. Over time, we plan to expand the ANGI Learning Profile and this personalized approach to learners of all ages and across all skills.

Does my child need a screen?+

No. Children learn through paper lessons, homework, and hands-on activities. Parents will also have a mobile web app to easily upload completed work and share feedback from their phones.

How are lessons personalized?+

AI uses your child’s ANGI Learning Profile to understand their growing skills, strengths, and areas that need support. It generates learning content across courses, connecting relevant skills and adapting what comes next to their progress.

Do I need to upload every piece of homework?+

No. You can upload completed work, answer simple yes-or-no questions about how your child got on, or do both. This feedback helps ANGI update their profile and adapt future learning content.

Is there a fixed schedule?+

No. Children can learn at their own pace, guided by their abilities and progress. ANGI adapts the next learning steps to what they are ready for, and your family chooses when to learn.

Is early access free?+

Yes. Access is currently free for early parents and participating partners.
