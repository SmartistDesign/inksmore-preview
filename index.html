02 / HOW IT WORKS

Get started in
three simple steps.

A little insight from you.
A learning path that grows with them.

When the course opens
Every practice
opens a new possibility.
  1. 1

    Tell us what they can do.

    Answer a short yes-or-no questionnaire to give ANGI a starting point.

  2. 2

    Share how the activity went.

    Print their math activities, then answer a few quick questions about how they got on.

  3. 3

    Let each practice shape the next.

    ANGI refines their learning profile with each update. Our AI uses it to tailor upcoming lessons and practice, including what to revisit and what to explore next.
